Who are We

HSAGP Energy LLC is a major development in Georgia's industrial sector. This joint venture company between Hyundai Motor Group and SK On has established an electric vehicle battery cell manufacturer covering 26,136,000 square feet at 5059 HWY 411, Kingston, Georgia 30145. Choosing this location shows our focus on efficiency and innovation, with easy access to HMG's vehicle production site. Our factory will produce 50 million battery cells, supporting around 300,000 electric vehicles. As we prepare to fully launch in 2025, HSAGP Energy LLC is leading the electric vehicle movement, committed to promoting sustainable transportation and boosting the local economy.
