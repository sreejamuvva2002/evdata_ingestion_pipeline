Ascend Elements is building North America and Europe’s domestic supply of critical minerals through “urban mining” and innovative battery materials technologies. We produce high-performance, engineered battery materials using the valuable elements found in end-of-life batteries and manufacturing scrap. Our patented Hydro-to-Cathode® direct cathode precursor (pCAM) synthesis process transforms today’s waste into high-value, engineered materials for tomorrow’s most advanced lithium-ion batteries.
