Compliant Business Recycling Services in Atlanta, GA

Your Trusted Partner in Electronics, Solar, and Battery Recycling in Metro Atlanta

With over 30 years of experience and compliant recycling processes, First America provides secure, sustainable, and scalable recycling solutions for businesses across Atlanta and beyond. Whether you need to dispose of IT assets, decommission solar panels, or handle complex battery waste, our Atlanta facility is ready to support your compliance and sustainability goals.

Electronics Recycling in Atlanta

From old servers and desktops to networking gear and mobile devices, we help Atlanta businesses recycle electronic equipment based on your unique needs.

    • Data-Secure Disposal: Compliant processes protect your sensitive business data.
    • Maximum Recovery: Extract value from gold, silver, copper, and other recoverable materials.
    • Tailored Solutions: Whether you’re upgrading systems or closing an office, we provide logistics and recycling solutions that fit your schedule and security needs.
    • Eco-Responsible: Help reduce e-waste in landfills and meet your company’s ESG benchmarks.

Atlanta’s Trusted Partner in Solar Panel Recycling

Two Sustainable Paths:

Reuse or Recover
Outdated or damaged solar panels don’t have to go to waste. First America provides businesses with two recycling options to get the most out of aging PV systems:

Reuse: For eligible panels, retest and redeploy for off-grid or community use.

Recycle: Recover valuable materials like silicon, aluminum, glass, and copper for reuse in the supply chain.

As solar technology evolves and global reuse markets expand, First America remains at the forefront. We monitor trends in panel chemistry, design, and second-life applications to ensure our clients stay compliant and cost-effective. Our team combines decades of industry experience with forward-thinking strategy, helping you navigate shifting regulations, sustainability goals, and emerging opportunities. When you choose First America, you’re not just working with a recycler; you’re partnering with a market-savvy, results-driven partner.

Visit our solar panel recycling FAQ to learn more about our recycling services for your next decommissioning project.

Maximize Recovery, Minimize Risk – Battery Recycling in Atlanta

We recycle most battery types from lithium-ion and lead-acid to large-format EV batteries. Atlanta businesses trust us for:

Minimized Risk: Avoid hazards like fires and toxic leaks with safe handling.
Chemistry-Aware Solutions: Our experts understand the economics of batteries containing cobalt, nickel, and other valuable resources.
Recycling Compliance: Safe and sustainable handling, recycling, and downstream management of all e-waste, including solar panels.

Let us simplify your battery recycling program while helping you reduce logistics costs and increase recycling value from materials.
