Safe and Responsible Battery Recycling with
Fulton Metals Recycling

Batteries power our daily lives—from electronics and power tools to toys and appliances. But when they’re no longer useful, tossing them in the trash can be dangerous. At Fulton Metals Recycling, we provide a safe and environmentally responsible way to recycle a wide variety of household and commercial batteries.

Whether you’re a homeowner, contractor, or business, our no-appointment drop-off service makes battery recycling fast, easy, and compliant with all Georgia environmental regulations.

Why Battery Recycling Matters

Batteries contain valuable metals like lithium, nickel, and lead—but they also pose serious environmental
hazards if disposed of improperly. Recycling your batteries helps:

  • Prevent soil and water contamination
  • Reduce landfill waste
  • Conserve natural resources
  • Ensure safe handling of hazardous materials

🚫 Batteries We Do Not Accept

Some batteries require special handling or may not be accepted at our facility, including:

  • Leaking or damaged batteries
  • Mercury-containing batteries
  • Button Cell Batteries (watch/hearing aid style)
  • AAA, AA, C, D, and 9V Rechargeable, Lithium or Alkaline
  • Gas tanks or propane cylinders
